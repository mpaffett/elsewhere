## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a contemplative, tactile, and literary ethos designed to counter the slick, synthetic uniformity of conventional digital software. Rooted in the rich tradition of fine press book-arts, slow publishing, and archival stationery, the visual landscape evokes hand-bound notebooks, heavy laid rag paper, and relief letterpress printing.

The brand targets thoughtful creators, avid readers, researchers, and cultural practitioners who value stillness, deliberate pacing, and typographic dignity. The emotional response is centered around grounding warmth, intellectual calm, craftsmanship, and tactile permanence. 

Stylistically, the aesthetic combines classical editorial precision with tactile bookbinding structure. It rejects rounded modern app idioms and gloss in favor of crisp 0-radius framing, rule lines, measured proportions, and warm physical layering.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the chromatic chemistry of raw paper stock, mineral pigments, and oil-based letterpress ink. Stark, synthetic whites and clinical neutral blacks are excluded.

- **Paper Tiers (Backgrounds):** The foundational substrate is `paper-base` (`#FCF9F4`), a warm unbleached rag paper tone. Layered panels and interactive containers step down subtly in luminance through `paper-surface` (`#F4EFE6`) and `paper-elevated` (`#EDE5D8`), maintaining tactile richness without cold gray shadows.
- **Inks (Typography & Rules):** All typography and structural rulings utilize `ink-charcoal` (`#2B2725`) and `ink-deep` (`#1D1916`), simulating matte soot and lampblack inks. Secondary text uses `ink-muted` (`#68615A`) for legible, non-aggressive optical hierarchy.
- **Pigments (Accents):** The primary color is `terracotta-rich` (`#C85A32`), evocative of warm terracotta tiles and vermilion editorial marginalia, shifting to `terracotta-deep` (`#B84A28`) on pressed interactions. Secondary pigment `ochre-warm` (`#D49B5B`) functions as a highlighter and secondary focus point. Tertiary accents `sage-soft` (`#84937E`) and `blush-petal` (`#DEB7A6`) offer archival notation tones for tags, badges, and contextual alerts.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is grounded in a double-serif architecture, pairing the classical, Aldine proportions of `EB Garamond` with the modern digital craftsmanship and open reading cadence of `Literata`.

- **Headlines (`EB Garamond`):** Used exclusively for titles, section heads, and editorial pullquotes. Its organic contrast and historic humanistic rhythm ground the page in quiet contemplation. For large scales, tighter leading maintains the feel of hand-set metal type.
- **Body & Labels (`Literata`):** Rendered in `Literata` to ensure sustained legibility across reading sessions. The open counters, sturdy horizontal serifs, and warm proportions allow smooth line tracking.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Small functional labels leverage `Literata` in small-cap treatment or slightly tracked uppercase to emulate vintage bibliographical notations and running book headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the classical book-arts grid: disciplined internal margins surrounded by generous, meditative external borders. The spatial cadence is paced, avoiding the frantic density of productivity software.

- **Grid Architecture:** A symmetrical 12-column layout on desktop (`max-width: 1280px`), adapting to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. Text essays and central reading surfaces sit within constrained single-column measure (60–75 characters per line, roughly 680px maximum).
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a 4px base increment expanding into generous multiples. Structural section gaps (`space-2xl` and `margin`) frame content blocks like page folios.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Full margins (`3rem`), generous structural gutters (`1.5rem`), dual-margin balance.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Standard margins (`2rem`), gutters (`1.25rem`), side notes collapsible to sequential blocks.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Tightened margin (`1.25rem`), gutter (`1rem`), single-column vertical flow with explicit structural horizontal rules separating distinct entries.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation does not rely on diffused synthetic drop shadows or floating elevations. Instead, physical presence is established through **tonal paper layering**, **crisp hairline borders**, and **stamped physical offset**:

1. **Substrate Layering:** Depth is primarily established by placing `paper-surface` or `paper-elevated` panels over `paper-base`. The visual shift is organic and non-luminescent.
2. **Hairline Framing:** All structural boundaries, dividers, and surface splits use sharp 1px borders in `border-subtle` (`#E2D9CB`) or `border-strong` (`#BDB2A2`). 
3. **Double-Rule & Oxford Borders:** Important focal cards, callouts, or editorial headers use an inner and outer rule (a 2px primary border nested with a 1px secondary border separated by 3px of blank space) reminiscent of bound book covers.
4. **Physical Letterpress Pressed Depth:** Interactive states do not lift up toward the viewer; rather, they press down. Pressed buttons shift 1px down and right, mimicking the mechanical bite of metal type into soft, damp cotton paper.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is strictly **sharp (`roundedness: 0`)**. 

Corners are meticulously uncurved across all interface items—from primary actions to cards, dialogs, inputs, and chips. This absolute angularity mirrors the cut edge of heavy paper, folio folios, book cloth binders, and engraved plates. 

Visual softness is generated entirely through the warmth of the color palette, the calligraphic curves of `EB Garamond`, and the generous white space—never via softened corner geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `terracotta-rich` (`#C85A32`) background, crisp 0-radius corners, `paper-base` typography (`Literata`, `label-md`), 1px solid `terracotta-deep` outline. Hover transitions deepen the tone to `terracotta-deep`. Active state drops 1px down with no elevation shadow.
- **Secondary (Editorial Outline):** Transparent background, 1px solid `ink-charcoal` border, `ink-charcoal` text. On hover, fills with `paper-surface`.
- **Tertiary (Text/Bookplate):** No border. Underlined with a 1px hairline rule spaced 4px below text baseline.

### Cards & Folios
- Constructed with sharp 90-degree corners and encased in 1px `border-subtle`.
- Background set to `paper-surface`.
- Optional archival header rule: a single horizontal line separating the card title from the body.
- No floating blur shadows; optional 2px hard solid offset shadow in `border-strong` for featured book cards.

### Input Fields & Textareas
- Sharp rectangular boxes framed by a 1px border in `border-strong`.
- Background in `paper-base` or slightly indented `paper-elevated`.
- Text renders in `ink-deep` using `Literata`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to 1.5px solid `terracotta-rich` with a 2px inner padding shift; no fuzzy blue glow.

### Chips & Badges
- Strict rectilinear containers with 0-radius corners.
- Subtle tinted backgrounds using `sage-soft` (for status or nature tags), `blush-petal` (for subjective annotations), or `paper-elevated` (neutral).
- Borders are 1px solid matching the tint's deeper tone.
- Typography styled in `label-sm` with letter-spacing for clean typographic stamp feel.

### Lists & Tables
- Divided exclusively by horizontal hairline rules in `border-subtle`. No alternating row zebra colors.
- Numerical lists use classic old-style figures where available, providing historic typographic cadence.
- Row hover states tint gently to `paper-surface`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** Sharp square box (16px × 16px), 1px border in `ink-muted`. Checked state fills with `terracotta-rich` featuring a crisp geometric cross or fill mark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Geometric sharp diamonds or precise concentric circles with a centered solid ink dot when active, echoing manual draftsmanship.

### Bookmarks & Marginalia Tabs
- Anchored to the edge of surfaces, styled with sharp geometric tab profiles, rendered in `ochre-warm` or `blush-petal`, bringing physical bookbinding metaphors directly to the digital workspace.
