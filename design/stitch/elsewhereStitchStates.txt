### 1. APPLICATION / DATA STATE (Model)
The page manages 4 core state variables:
- `screenTimeMinutes`: number (default: 225, i.e., 3h 45m). Range: 90m (1h 30m) to 420m+ (7h 00m).
- `selectedVessel`: "drawing" | "journaling" | "poetry" (default: "drawing" / "The Daily Object").
- `selectedFloor`: 15 | 25 | 45 (default: 25).
- `checkoutStatus`: "idle" | "submitting" | "success" | "error" (default: "idle").

---

### 2. COMPONENT-BY-COMPONENT INTERACTION STATES

#### Step 1: Screen Time Slider (`screenTimeMinutes`)
- **Default / Initial State:** Slider set to 3h 45m (the UK/US average).
  - Stat readout displays: `3h 45m / day`
  - Computed tag displays: `= 26 hrs wasted this week` (formula: `(minutes * 7) / 60` rounded).
  - Helper copy displays: `"Taking just 25 min leaves you with plenty of day"` (or updates dynamically to match selected floor).
- **Active / Dragging State:**
  - Thumb: terracotta circle with white/cream inner ring, subtle shadow, cursor `grabbing`.
  - Track: filled terracotta line up to thumb position; remaining track is muted warm cream-gray.
  - Live recalculation of both the daily time and weekly wasted hours.
- **Tick / Anchor Marks:** Labels for `1h 30m (Light)`, `2h 45m`, `3h 45m (UK/US Average)`, `5h 15m`, `7h 00m+ (Heavy)` with visual notch markers.

#### Step 2: Creative Vessel Cards (`selectedVessel`)
Three cards:
1. *Discipline I • Drawing: The Daily Object* (with pencil/drafting icon)
2. *Discipline II • Journaling: The Seven Prompts* (with notebook/quill icon)
3. *Discipline III • Poetry: The Guided Poem* (with quotation mark icon)

- **Selected State (Active):**
  - Border: 2px solid terracotta (`#C85A32`).
  - Background: soft warm cream/tint (`#FFF8F5`).
  - Badge: solid terracotta pill with white uppercase text `"SELECTED"` in top-right corner.
  - Text & icon: highlighted warm terracotta accents.
- **Unselected / Idle State (Inactive):**
  - Border: 1px solid subtle warm border (`#E1D8D5`).
  - Background: pure white or container-low.
  - No "SELECTED" badge.
  - Links: `"Inspect Vessel"` in muted warm ink.
- **Hover State:**
  - Subtle warm border darkening, slight elevation or 1px translateY lift, cursor `pointer`.
- **Focus-Visible (Keyboard navigation):**
  - 2px terracotta outline with 2px offset for accessibility.

#### Step 3: Daily Floor Threshold (`selectedFloor`)
Three choice cards:
1. `15 min` — "GENTLE FLOOR"
2. `25 min` — "RECOMMENDED"
3. `45 min` — "DEEP FOCUS"

- **Selected State:**
  - Border: 2px solid terracotta (`#C85A32`).
  - Number text: highlighted in terracotta, bold serif.
  - Label: terracotta sub-label (e.g., `"RECOMMENDED"`).
- **Unselected State:**
  - Border: 1px solid subtle warm border (`#E1D8D5`).
  - Number text: deep charcoal ink (`#2B2725`).
  - Subtitle: muted warm gray uppercase label.
- **Hover State:**
  - Border transitions to warm ochre/terracotta tint, cursor `pointer`.

#### Step 4: Checkout Bar & Summary Line
- **Dynamic Summary Pill State:**
  - Text dynamically reflects active choices:
    `Selected plan: [Vessel Name] at a [15/25/45]-minute daily quiet floor`
    (e.g., `"Selected plan: The Daily Object at a 25-minute daily quiet floor"`).
  - Price: `Single dispatch • £9 one-time`.

- **Email Input Field:**
  - **Empty / Placeholder State:** `"Enter your quiet email for morning folios..."` in muted gray.
  - **Focused State:** 1.5px warm terracotta border ring, soft off-white fill, cursor active.
  - **Filled / Valid State:** Deep ink text, valid format.
  - **Invalid / Error State:** Soft terracotta/crimson border, inline subtle error notice (`"Please enter a valid email address"`).

- **Submit Button (`Begin 7-Day Plan — £9 →`):**
  - **Default / Idle:** Terracotta background (`#C85A32`), white serif/sans text with right arrow icon.
  - **Hover:** Darker rich rust (`#A84724`), smooth transition.
  - **Active / Pressed:** `transform: translateY(1px)`.
  - **Loading / Submitting:** Button disabled, text replaced with quiet spinner or `"Preparing your folios..."`.
  - **Success:** Redirects to Stripe Checkout or displays confirmation message (`"Check your inbox for dawn dispatch"`).

#### Navigation & Header Links:
- **Active Navigation Link:** Underlined with 1px terracotta rule (`1. Reclaimed Time`).
- **Inactive Links:** Muted warm gray, hover to charcoal ink.
- **Founder Pill (Top Right):** Matt's circular avatar + `"Made by Matt • Founder's note"` (hover underlines link).
